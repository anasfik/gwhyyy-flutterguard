FlutterGuard/Contact

Contact FlutterGuard

One maintainer, three channels, each for a different kind of message.

Bugs and feature requests

Use GitHub issues (opens in a new tab). Include the app version, the Android version and the device model, plus the smallest reproduction you can manage. If a scan failed, say which step failed and what the artifact was: an installed app, a split APK, a single APK or an AAB. Do not attach an APK you are not allowed to share, and do not paste credentials or personal data from a report.

Security reports

Report a vulnerability in FlutterGuard itself by email to security@gwhyyy.com. The disclosure policy, scope and contact details are published in security.txt. Do not open a public issue for an unfixed vulnerability.

Everything else

For licensing, partnerships, press or a question that does not fit the two channels above, write to work@gwhyyy.com. This is a small project, so replies are best-effort; a specific message with a link or a reproduction is answered faster than a general one.

What not to send

FlutterGuard is a static inspection tool. Do not send a request to test a third-party app or an endpoint you do not own. Authorisation is the boundary between an audit and an intrusion, and the project will not help cross it. For questions about interpreting a finding, describe the artifact and the evidence and leave out live secrets.

Privacy

Email and GitHub exchanges are handled by those providers under their own policies. The app itself sends nothing. The website's data handling is described on privacy and data handling.
