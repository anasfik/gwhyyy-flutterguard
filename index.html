Look inside a Flutter APK.

Inspect an installed Flutter app or a single APK. Read its libraries, endpoints and exposed strings, then save the report on your device.

Download APK
Not yet available on Google Play

Android 8+ · Offline by design · MIT

What it does

  • Discover

    Find Flutter apps already installed, or pick a single APK.

  • Inspect

    Read libraries, endpoints and exposed strings from the release.

  • Report

    Save the evidence on the phone; nothing is uploaded.

Why it stays local

  • Offline by design

    No INTERNET permission, so scan data cannot leave the device.

  • Open source

    MIT code you can read, build and question.

  • No account

    No sign-up, no sync, no analytics. Open it and scan.
